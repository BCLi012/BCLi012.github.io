Reflection

Modern interfaces are optimized for engagement; regaining attention requires deliberate constraints: scheduled email checks, phone-free windows, and minimalist interfaces.

Practical tips

- Use discrete notification settings
- Timebox social media
- Practice short daily writing to clarify thoughts
