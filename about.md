I'm **Bruce Li (Bingcheng Li)**. The name Bruce comes from two people I keep returning to: **Bruce Lee**,
whose "be water" is less a slogan than a working method, and **Bruce Liang (梁博)**,
whose patience with a single song is a reminder that depth beats breadth.

This site is where those two instincts meet — engineering projects and research
notes on one side, essays and records of the outdoors on the other.

## What you'll find here

- **Projects** — things I've built, and what I learned building them
- **Research** — papers, notes, and open questions
- **Arts** — photography, film notes, and other creative work
- **Sports** — climbing, hiking, and time outside
- **Thoughts** — essays on technology, work, and attention
