**Course project** · May – October 2024

Through coursework and independent practice, I used R and Python to study operations-management topics with machine learning — model selection and evaluation, regression and prediction, classification and clustering, dimensionality reduction and evaluation metrics.

## Predicting stock price movements on the STAR Market

Using historical price data from China's STAR Market, I reduced features with principal component analysis (PCA), then predicted future price movements with logistic regression, SVM, and random forest — and compared the performance of the three approaches.

## What drives video game ratings

Starting from a Kaggle dataset, I applied text analysis to identify the factors that significantly affect video game ratings, built neural-network prediction models on top of those factors, and used clustering to build user-segment profiles.
