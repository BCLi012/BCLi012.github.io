**President** · September 2024 – June 2025
Wuhan University Logistics and Supply Chain Management Association

## Academic forum on management science and engineering

As project coordinator I helped faculty host the forum, inviting Prof. Zheng Quan (University of Science and Technology of China) and Assoc. Prof. Wang Yunjie (Renmin University of China) to speak on behavioral pricing, shopping-cashback mechanism design, and strategic consumer behavior. More than 150 faculty and students attended.

## Industry frontier lectures

I planned and organized a lecture series bringing senior experts from JD.com, Haier, and Lam Research to share hands-on practice and industry trends. The series reached over 1,000 students and faculty, building a bridge between academia and industry.
