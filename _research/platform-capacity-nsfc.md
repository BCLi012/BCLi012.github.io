**Research assistant** · since June 2024
NSFC General Program (No. 72171178) · Advisor: Prof. Bin Dai
*Capacity-Constrained Channel Decisions, Capacity Allocation, and Pricing for Retail Platform Supply Chains with Strategic Consumers*

## What the project studies

How a retail platform makes channel, capacity-allocation, and pricing decisions when capacity is constrained and consumers behave strategically — and how information disclosure can be designed when the platform holds private information.

## Training and activities

- **Operations research camp** — modeling under strategic consumer behavior; applying signaling games and passive-belief equilibrium to information-disclosure problems under asymmetric information; working with LaTeX and Mathematica. Presented a replication of *Disclosing Endogenous Cost Information* (Xu Jiang, Yan Xiong, *The Accounting Review*), including the reordered-game solution approach.
- **Weekly research group** — read 20+ papers from the UTD-24 list, covering CSR operations, strategic consumers, endogenous signaling games, and AI. Framed research questions from everyday observations and reviewed the quality-signaling literature.
- **Optimization training** — convex optimization foundations, nonlinear programming via KKT conditions, and traveling-salesman problems solved with Gurobi. Presented *Optimal Retail Location: Empirical Methodology and Application to Practice* (Marshall Fisher et al., *M&SOM*).
